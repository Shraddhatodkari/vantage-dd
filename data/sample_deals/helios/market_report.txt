MARKET NOTE — EMBEDDED PAYMENTS & PAYFAC SEGMENT

Embedded payments volume is forecast to grow at an 18% CAGR through 2030.
Take rates are compressing by 5-10 basis points per year as large platforms internalise payments.
High-risk verticals (travel, nutraceuticals, gaming) carry chargeback ratios 2-3x the card-not-present average and attract enhanced network monitoring programmes.
Comparable PayFac transactions have been priced at 3x to 5x revenue for compliant, low-risk portfolios.
