NORTHWIND LOGISTICS — MANAGEMENT PRESENTATION (CONFIDENTIAL, PREPARED FOR PROSPECTIVE ACQUIRERS)

Section 1: Investment Highlights
Northwind Logistics is a leading mid-market freight and last-mile logistics provider operating 42 distribution hubs across the Midwest and Southeast United States.
- Revenue grew 40% year-over-year in FY2025, reflecting strong organic momentum and new enterprise wins.
- Industry-leading EBITDA margin of 22% driven by our proprietary route-optimisation platform.
- Highly diversified customer base; no single customer represents a material share of revenue.
- Clean balance sheet with conservative leverage and ample covenant headroom.
- The company is not party to any material litigation.

Section 2: Growth Strategy
Management plans to open 8 new hubs in FY2026 funded from operating cash flow, and to expand the cold-chain segment which commands premium pricing.
The route-optimisation platform reduced empty miles by 14% in FY2025.

Section 3: Management Team
CEO Daniel Okafor (joined 2019), CFO Priya Raman (joined 2024, previous CFO departed in Q3 2023), COO Mark Lindqvist.
