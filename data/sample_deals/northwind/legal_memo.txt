OUTSIDE COUNSEL MEMORANDUM — PENDING AND THREATENED LITIGATION (PRIVILEGED & CONFIDENTIAL)

1. Ramirez et al. v. Northwind Logistics (filed 2025, N.D. Ill.). Putative class action by approx. 1,900 current and former drivers alleging misclassification as independent contractors and unpaid overtime. Plaintiffs seek damages estimated at $12 million plus attorneys' fees. Counsel assesses the likelihood of an adverse outcome as reasonably possible. No reserve has been recorded.

2. Environmental notice of violation, Georgia hub (2024). A diesel spill at the Atlanta hub resulted in a notice of violation; remediation cost estimated at $0.8 million. Indemnity from landlord is disputed.

3. Data incident. In August 2024 an unauthorised party accessed a customer portal and obtained shipping records. Contact for the incident response lead: jordan.reyes@northwind-logistics.example, phone (312) 555-0187. Regulatory notifications were filed in four states.
