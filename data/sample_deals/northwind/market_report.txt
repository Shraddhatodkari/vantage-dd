INDEPENDENT MARKET STUDY — US MID-MARKET FREIGHT & LAST-MILE LOGISTICS (COMMISSIONED BY BUY-SIDE ADVISOR)

The addressable US mid-market freight and last-mile logistics segment is estimated at $58 billion in 2025 and is forecast to grow at a 6% CAGR through 2030.
Cold-chain logistics is the fastest growing sub-segment at an 11% CAGR.
The market is fragmented; the top five players hold approximately 22% share. Northwind is estimated to hold 0.5% share.
Key competitive threats include large carriers moving downmarket and digital freight brokers compressing pricing. Pricing pressure of 2-4% per year is expected in contract freight.
Typical EBITDA margins for comparable mid-market operators range from 11% to 15%.
Recent comparable transactions were priced at 7.5x to 9.0x EBITDA.
