NORTHWIND LOGISTICS — FY2025 SUSTAINABILITY & GOVERNANCE SUMMARY

Environmental: Scope 1 emissions of 184,000 tCO2e, up 12% versus FY2024 due to fleet expansion. Only 3% of the fleet is electric or alternative fuel. No science-based emissions target has been set.
Social: Driver turnover was 61% in FY2025 (industry average approx. 45%). Two recordable serious safety incidents.
Governance: The board has five members, two of whom are independent. The CFO role changed in 2024. The audit committee is chaired by a non-independent director. The FY2025 audit opinion was unqualified, but the auditor noted a material weakness in revenue recognition controls relating to manual journal entries.
